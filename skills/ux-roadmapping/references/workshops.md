# Roadmapping workshops

Independently summarized from the NN/g UX Roadmaps Workshop Cheat Sheet (Sara Paul, 2026). Read the method reference for context. Timings below are the handout's examples, not mandatory meeting lengths.

## Pick the format

Use the one-workshop format when goals and useful inputs are already available. Use the two-workshop format when alignment or evidence gaps need work before prioritization. If the user has less time, propose a shortened agenda and name what must happen asynchronously; do not silently claim it covers the full course exercise.

## One workshop: three hours

Before: establish the roadmap's purpose and gather research, artifacts and stakeholder perspectives. Identify creator, contributors and consumers and send an input pack with source IDs.

| Activity | Minutes | Useful result |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction, icebreaker, hopes/fears | 25 | Shared purpose and concerns |
| Context and input-method readout | 15 | Common evidence baseline |
| Distill themes: individual ideas, small-group convergence, playback | 45 | Candidate needs/outcomes tied to inputs |
| Break | 20 | |
| Whole-group affinity mapping and theme naming | 30 | Shared theme structure with leftovers |
| Agree criteria, prioritize and play back | 45 | Proposed order and reasons |

After: visualize the roadmap, write a recap, choose audience/sharing approach and arrange a revisit. Distribution is a proposed next step unless the user authorizes sending.

## Two workshops with a research interval

Workshop 1, one hour:

| Activity | Minutes | Useful result |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction and roadmap definition | 5 | Shared framing |
| Individual and converged hopes/fears | 15 | Primary purpose |
| Strategy collection with stakeholders/knowledge holders | 30 | Strategic input and candidate patterns |
| Open questions | 10 | Research gaps |

Between: fill relevant gaps through user, competitive or secondary research and stakeholder/expert interviews. Propose a research plan, owners and decision dates without implying interviews happened or authorizing external contact. Use already available evidence first.

Workshop 2, two hours:

| Activity | Minutes | Useful result |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction and recap | 15 | Updated evidence baseline |
| Distill themes in small groups and play back | 30 | Candidate themes |
| Break | 15 | |
| Affinity mapping and naming | 30 | Agreed structure |
| Agree criteria, prioritize and play back | 30 | Proposed priorities and tradeoffs |

After: visualize, recap, decide sharing strategy and revisit.

## Facilitation adaptations

These are practical additions, not verbatim course rules:
- Label solution requests separately from needs; use the Confidence Reframe when needed.
- Let people generate input individually before converging so senior voices do not define all themes.
- Ask participants to show source IDs and distinguish evidence from preference.
- Keep dissent, unresolved choices and ungrouped inputs visible.
- End with a draft owner, decisions needed, gaps and next review. A workshop vote does not by itself create a delivery commitment.
